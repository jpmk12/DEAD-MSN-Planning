Q2 · Millilux display.
Show computed clear-sky mlx + LOW/HIGH badge + cloud caveat (recommended) Show only disk % + moon altitude + LOW/HIGH call (no mlx number)
Q3 · Activation.
Only when the “NVG sortie” toggle is on (recommended) Always show a slim illumination line on any night phase
Q4 · LOW illum severity.
Caution-colored chip/badge, no effect on GO/NO-GO (recommended) Purely informational (neutral color) Contributes CAUTION to the phase status
